# Image analysis to prompt

## Visible evidence

Describe only what can be seen:

- subject and action
- environment
- framing and spatial relationship
- light direction and contrast
- colour and texture
- visible constraints

## Classification

For shot size, angle, composition, perspective, focus, and lighting, state:

- selected term
- confidence: high, medium, or low
- visible reason

## Uncertain or unverifiable

List attributes that cannot be recovered from the image alone, such as exact lens, camera body, movement, or intent.

## Reproducible prompt

Translate the visible evidence into one coherent generation prompt.
