# Quick prompt template

## Model-ready prompt

```text
[subject and visible action] in [environment and time].
[shot size], [camera angle], [composition and spatial relationship].
[perspective feel], [depth of field and focus target].
[motivated lighting source and contrast], [colour and texture].
Keep [identity, geometry, text, product, or brand constraints] consistent.
Avoid [specific likely failure modes].
```

## Assumptions

Include only if a reversible assumption was necessary.
