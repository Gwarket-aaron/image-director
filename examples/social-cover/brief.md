# Synthetic example: editorial social cover

## Brief

Create a 16:9 editorial cover about a small team reviewing an AI workflow.

Requirements:

- two anonymous people reviewing a process map on paper
- quiet space in the upper-left for a headline
- realistic workplace, not a science-fiction control room
- restrained blue accents
- faces are not the focus

## Expected decisions

- medium-wide shot to keep people and process visible
- slight high angle to make the paper workflow readable
- subjects placed to the right to preserve headline space
- soft window light, neutral surfaces, restrained blue accent
- no holograms, glowing interfaces, or illegible decorative text
