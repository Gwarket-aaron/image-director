# Privacy

Image Director itself:

- does not collect telemetry
- does not scan your computer
- does not upload files
- does not store prompts outside the folder

The AI client or image-generation provider you use may process your prompt, uploaded image, and generated output. Check that provider's data retention and training settings before using confidential material.

Avoid including:

- credentials or access tokens
- private client information
- unpublished product details
- faces or personal data without an appropriate basis
